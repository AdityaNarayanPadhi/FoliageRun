Open Innovation Manifesto

TOUCH GRASS IN BHUBANESWAR.

FoliageRun was conceived as an open-source answer to modern fitness tracking fatigue. Instead of keeping you glued to screens, badges, and ads, our AI agent dispatches you outside in under 30 seconds.

01

Zero Telemetry Surveillance

Commercial fitness applications hoard and sell runners' exact residential coordinates, daily pacing habits, and biometrics. FoliageRun processes route synthesis on decentralized cloud weights with zero user tracking.

02

Open Geospatial Commons

Our route engine is built exclusively on volunteer OpenStreetMap data and Overpass API query vectors. No corporate tollbooths or paywalled greenways.

03

Mastra Agent Architecture

Powered by the Mastra AI agent framework, pairing open-weight LLMs with live foliage intelligence and Overpass vector queries for real-time autumn route synthesis.
